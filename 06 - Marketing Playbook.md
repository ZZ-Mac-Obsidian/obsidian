# Marketing Playbook

## How players actually arrive

- Over 90% of play starts from the Home page (official, 2025). The tile is the advert.
- About 40% of the top 100 recommended games each week are under three months old (Roblox, Q3 2026). New games do get shown.
- Hits spread through creators and short video: Dress to Impress through a TikTok trend, Dead Rails through Roblox's own social posts and YouTubers (third-party).

## The tile

The full standard is [[17 - Icons Thumbnails and CTR]]. In brief:

- **Icon:** one subject filling most of the square, a big emotion, readable at 150 pixels.
- **Thumbnails:** three to five, each selling a different promise, personalisation on. Each shows the verb and the twist.
- **Video:** the core action in the first two seconds, no spoken audio.
- **Name:** the verb and the noun, with an update tag in brackets.
- **Honesty:** a click that bounces within a minute counts against the game, so show what is really there.

## Paid ads

- Sponsored tiles on Home, bought in Ads Manager with ad credits. Roblox reports under $0.01 per join and that over 70% of the top 100 run ads (official).
- Start small, targeted at new players, age-checked where possible, to reach the highly-engaged-play threshold.
- **Players who first arrive by an ad never count toward Home recommendation signals** (official). Ads buy data and the threshold, not ranking. Do not scale spend until the day-14 gates pass.

## Free reach

- **Short video:** before-and-after clips of the core action for TikTok, Shorts and Roblox Moments. Use the share link in every post.
- **Milestone codes:** "5K likes", "25K members" codes that pay a little in-game currency, as Clean the Moon does. Keep rewards out of the game description.
- **Group:** small in-game reward for joining; post each update there.
- **Creators:** send a private server link and a one-line pitch to mid-size Roblox YouTubers once the game holds players.
- **Notifications:** opt-in, 13+, one per player per day at most (official). Use for the daily job or a new area, never for guilt.

## Update rhythm

- Fixed day each week, Saturday if possible.
- Put the update name in the title tag and a new thumbnail.
- Each update adds; never wipe or devalue old progress.
- Full rhythm, events and what to read in the numbers: [[22 - Live Ops and Updates]].

## Picking the idea

Do this before any build. Detail in [[09 - Slop Game Research]].

1. Search the fantasy in Roblox's Creator Exchange or a tracker, sorted by visits.
2. Look for many players spread over few or weak games.
3. Check demand off Roblox: Steam wishlists, web-game views.
4. Confirm a current hit already uses the mechanic.
5. Write down the one twist that makes this version different.

## Riding a trend

- Watch Top Trending and Up-and-Coming for a new core action with a high rating.
- The factory advantage is speed: skeleton ready, only theme and action to build.
- Change the setting, look and payoff. Near-duplicates get less reach (official) and Steal a Brainrot's owners have sued clones (third-party).
- Trend windows are weeks. Clean the Supermarket went from 62k players to about 300 in four months.
