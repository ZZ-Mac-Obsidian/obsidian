## Not done or not verified

- **Nothing in this vault has been tested in a published game.** It is research and standards, not results.
- Of the two YouTube videos originally linked, the first was read through a third-party auto-transcript; the second could not be read at all. No further YouTube videos were watched in the 11 Nov pass; video-derived advice comes from creator transcripts and the reference vaults.
- [[09 - Slop Game Research]] rests on creators' own claims. Earnings figures are unverified, and several creators sell courses or are sponsored.
- The two downloaded reference vaults are largely AI-generated and self-labelled as untested.
- **Which countries and ages restrict paid random items:** Roblox's page does not list them; it says to ask the policy service per player. An earlier list in this vault (UK and Brazil under-18s, Belgium, Netherlands, Australia) is unconfirmed.
- **The under-16 publishing fee amount** was not on the page read; the threshold read was 250 unique plays by highly engaged age-checked users in 60 days. It has changed several times.
- **Mesh triangle cap:** sources give 10,000 and about 20,000. Check the current specifications page.
- **Developer Exchange rate** of $0.0038 per Robux and the 30% platform fee come from third-party sources that agree; the official pages read did not state them.
- **AI 3D tool free tiers and licence terms** conflict between sources and change often.
- **Whether Roblox discounts idle time** beyond the 60-minute daily cap: not stated anywhere official.
- Benchmarks for play-through rate and payer share are third-party.
- Game and character names were checked by web search, not on Roblox itself.
- EU Digital Fairness Act: only a leaked draft was available.
