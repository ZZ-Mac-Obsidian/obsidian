# Build Prompt Template

The brief to hand to the AI builder (written for Claude Opus 5.5). Copy everything below the line, fill in every {blank}, and start the session in this vault's folder so the builder can read the notes. [[Games/Scrub Club]] is a complete worked example.

Leave a blank as "your call" where the builder should decide. A good brief fixes the twist, the audience and the constraints, and leaves names, character designs and flourishes open.

---

You are building a complete, polished Roblox game with a solo developer. I will play every milestone and give feedback, so the game must always be in a state I can press Play on.

## What I want

A game that a player clicks because the tile is exciting and honest, understands in five seconds, enjoys on the first input, is rewarded every minute or two for the first fifteen, wants to return to tomorrow, and can spend in without being pushed. It should look and sound finished: bouncy interface, satisfying sounds, characters with personality, rarity you can feel.

The standards for all of that are in this vault. They explain why as well as what, so when you meet a decision they do not cover, choose what best serves the reasons. Where a note gives numbers, treat them as starting points to tune by playing. Where you can justify something better than the standard, tell me and do the better thing.

You have creative freedom over names, character designs, palette, music, jokes and the signature flourishes that make this game its own. Make it funny and distinctive. Do not make it look like the game it was inspired by.

## Before you start

1. List the tools you can drive in this session (Roblox Studio, Blender, web search, image, 3D, audio or interface generation) and what each can and cannot do. If you cannot drive Studio or Blender, switch to giving me scripts and steps to run.
2. Search Roblox for the name "{Game Name}" and for games with the same premise. If taken, use "{Backup Name}" or propose three and ask.
3. Roblox changes often. Before relying on any engine feature, discovery rule or monetization policy, check the current page on create.roblox.com rather than memory or this vault.
4. Tell me your plan for step 1 in a few lines, then begin.

## How to work

- **Follow [[21 - Build Order]] and stop at each gate.** Tell me what works, what does not, what you tested and what you could not test, then wait for me to play.
- **Verify by playing, and by looking.** After each change run the game in Studio, including a phone-sized screen. Take screenshots of interfaces, models and effects and judge them by eye against the standards. Read the output console for errors.
- **Feel goes in from step 1.** Sound, motion and numbers are part of proving the loop is fun, not polish for later.
- **Time the first fifteen minutes** from a fresh save at every milestone from step 8 on, against the early-wins table.
- **Keep everything tunable.** Prices, rates, timings, rarity weights and theme colours live in shared config.
- **Server decides everything that matters.** Clients send intentions.
- **Ask before** anything that spends Robux, money or paid generation credits, publishes the game, uploads assets to my account, or changes account settings.
- **Licences:** only assets cleared for commercial use; record each in `credits.md` when added. Nothing taken from another game, show or brand. Invent the characters.
- **Keep a short state note** in the game folder: done, in progress, known issues, decisions and why. Update it at each gate so a new session can pick up.
- **If you are stuck or a tool fails,** diagnose it and try another route before asking. If something in the brief is impossible or a bad idea, say so early.

## Done means

- The tile checklist in [[17 - Icons Thumbnails and CTR]] passes at small size beside real competitors.
- A new player on a phone is acting within 5 seconds and hits every row of the early-wins table.
- The core action is fun for two minutes with nothing else in the game.
- Rarity is obvious with the sound off, and with eyes closed.
- Closing the game for an hour and returning is rewarding.
- After a week of play there are still goals.
- Every shop item says exactly what it gives; nothing pressures or misleads; a free player can reach everything.
- Steady frame rate on a low-end phone with a full server.
- Every item on [[05 - Publish Checklist]] is ticked or handed to me.
