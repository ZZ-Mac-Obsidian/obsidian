# Genre Playbooks

The kinds of game that chart on Roblox, and how each plugs into [[01 - Game Standard]]. Pick one here after the demand check in [[09 - Slop Game Research]].

**The fast meme genres ("+1" stat, "Steal a ...", hazard runs, roll for rarity, tappers, grow and wait) have full blueprints with numbers in [[20 - Slop Game Blueprints]].** This note gives the wider map and one paragraph on each genre.

**Sources:** player figures are third-party tracker data from Nov 2026 research. "Factory fit" is my judgement of how well one person with AI tools can build and maintain it, not a measured fact. Check the Charts before choosing; what is rising changes weekly.

## At a glance

| Genre | Chart examples | Session | Factory fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collect and upgrade simulator | Clean the Moon, Sweep The Moon, Barber Simulator | 15 to 25 min | High |
| Steal and collect tycoon | Steal an Egg, Steal a Brainrot | 20+ min | High, but crowded |
| Grow and idle | Grow a Garden | Short visits, many a day | High |
| One-stat escape | +1 Speed Keyboard Escape, Ride A Pet | 5 to 15 min | High, short-lived |
| Feed, throw or build toy | Throw Food For Animals, Build a Bridge | 10 to 20 min | High |
| Luck and collection | Fish It, Fisch, Sol's RNG | Long, often idle | Medium |
| Physics sandbox | Ragdoll Physics, Fling Things and People | 5 to 15 min | Medium |
| Obby and escape | Do Not Stop, 13 Days to Escape, Tower of Hell | 5 to 20 min | Medium |
| Co-op survival | 99 Nights in the Forest, Dead Rails | 20 to 40 min | Medium to low |
| Round-based social | Murder Mystery 2, Dress to Impress | 6 min rounds | Medium to low |
| One-versus-all horror | Forsaken, Dandy's World | 10 min rounds | Low |
| Tower defense | Tower Defense Simulator | 20 min | Low |
| Fighting and action RPG | Blox Fruits, Jujutsu Shenanigans, Rivals | Long | Low |
| Roleplay and life sim | Brookhaven, Adopt Me | Long | Low |

Low fit means balance, anti-cheat, content volume or community management beyond one person, not that the genre is bad.

## Collect and upgrade simulator

- **Loop:** do a simple action, collect what it makes, deliver or sell, upgrade, unlock the next area.
- **First minute:** the action works at once and visibly changes the world.
- **Return:** offline machine, daily job, next area, collection.
- **Earns:** boosts, in-play products, cosmetics.
- **Risk:** players finish and leave. Cleaning games fell 95 to 99% in months without new content.
- **Example build:** [[Games/Scrub Club]].

## Steal and collect tycoon

- **Loop:** buy or hatch collectibles, place them on a base where they earn, upgrade, defend from thieves, rebirth.
- **First minute:** own one earning item and see another player's base.
- **Return:** income grew while away; fear of being robbed; Saturday events.
- **Earns:** random eggs, luck, shields. See the cautions in [[03 - Monetization and Shop Standard]].
- **Risk:** the original held about 92% of players across 105 clones; owners have sued copies; heavy cheating.
- **Look up:** `References/Filhao Roblox Vault/02 Game Design/Steal and Collect Tycoon Formula`.

## Grow and idle

- **Loop:** plant or place, wait, harvest, reinvest; stock rotates; things grow offline.
- **First minute:** plant, and see the first thing finish quickly.
- **Return:** timers finished; shop stock changed.
- **Earns:** speed-ups, rare stock, pets.
- **Risk:** criticised as pay-to-grow; record peaks fell fast between events.

## One-stat escape

- **Loop:** one number (speed, strength, size) rises with every action; gates open as it grows.
- **First minute:** the number is already climbing and the first gate opens.
- **Return:** weak. Add rebirth, pets and new worlds early.
- **Earns:** multipliers, auto-train, pets.
- **Risk:** burns out in weeks. Good for fast trend entries.

## Feed, throw or build toy

- **Loop:** a playful physical action (throw, stack, build) that pays out and unlocks bigger versions.
- **First minute:** the action is funny or surprising on the first try.
- **Return:** new animals, tools or levels; collection.
- **Earns:** in-play products, bigger tools, cosmetics.
- **Risk:** depends on one good toy. Prototype it alone first.

## Luck and collection

- **Loop:** roll, fish or open for items of rising rarity; fill an index; trade or show off.
- **First minute:** first catch or roll, with a rarity reveal.
- **Return:** incomplete index, limited items, trading.
- **Earns:** luck and auto passes. Luck sold for Robux counts as a paid random item under Roblox rules.
- **Risk:** regulation for minors; idle play that Roblox does not count past an hour.

## Physics sandbox

- **Loop:** no set goal; funny physics with friends, light unlocks.
- **First minute:** something ridiculous happens without instruction.
- **Return:** friends, new maps and toys.
- **Earns:** toys, maps, cosmetics; weakest earner here.
- **Risk:** hard to give long-term goals.

## Obby and escape

- **Loop:** get through stages; checkpoints; a clear end.
- **First minute:** first stage cleared.
- **Return:** new stages, speed records, a set number of days or floors to finish.
- **Earns:** skips, checkpoints, trails. Do not sell skips for stages made unfair on purpose.
- **Risk:** finished once, rarely replayed.

## Co-op survival

- **Loop:** gather, craft, survive nights or travel a route with friends toward a stated end.
- **First minute:** find a tool and a first resource; danger is clearly coming.
- **Return:** classes, a clear finish line, weekly updates. Best long-tail result of the 2025 hits.
- **Earns:** classes, revives, cosmetics.
- **Risk:** enemies, balance and content volume are a lot for one person.

## Round-based social

- **Loop:** short rounds with voting, hiding or deduction; rank up; show off.
- **First minute:** inside a round, not a lobby.
- **Return:** ranks, new items, friends.
- **Earns:** cosmetics almost entirely; praised as fair.
- **Risk:** needs enough players per server from day one.

## The harder genres

One-versus-all horror, tower defense, fighting, action RPG and roleplay hold the top of the charts for years, but they need character rosters, balance, anti-cheat or a living community. Treat them as a second-stage goal once a factory game is paying for itself.
